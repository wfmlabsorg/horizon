# ground-truth-daily.csv

Day-level latent variables of the generator — the ground truth the agent chain is supposed to recover. Not part of the book. `rho` is workload ÷ capacity in reference-AHT hours; `overflow_mult` multiplies voice contacts; `spillover_mult` multiplies contacts per transaction.

## Provenance

Generated by `sim/generate.ts` (seed 20260720); synthetic data with a recorded ground truth in `sim/GROUND-TRUTH.md`. Regenerate with `bun run sim/generate.ts`. Rows: 120.
