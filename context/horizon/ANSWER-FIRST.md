# Answer-First Standard

**Purpose:** The same output shape for a daily note, a question card and a plan. If the reader stops after the first line, they have the answer.

---

## The Shape

```markdown
# <Title sentence, with grade>

**What changed:** <one to three lines; each number graded and its definition cited>
**Decision requested:** <one line, or "none — for information">
**Next date:** <when the next update or readout is due>

---
<supporting detail, in the order a skeptic would ask for it>
```

---

## The Title Sentence

- States the finding, not the topic. "Voice AHT is up 40s since go-live and the shift is structural [C]" not "AHT update".
- Carries the grade of its load-bearing number.
- One sentence. No colon-then-explainer.

## What Changed

- Only what changed since the last note of this kind. Not a restatement.
- Every number graded (see GRADES.md).
- Every driver tagged structural or transitional.

## Decision Requested

- One line. Names the decision and who decides.
- If nothing is requested, say "none — for information" so silence is not ambiguous.

## Next Date

- The date the next version of this output is due, or the date a test will have data.

---

## Applied To

| Output | Title sentence answers | Decision requested is usually |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Daily note | Did yesterday land on forecast, and why not? | Approve the reforecast |
| Answer card | Why did X happen? | Accept the finding, or fund the test that would settle it |
| Weekly review | What did the week's misses have in common? | Refresh which assumptions |
| Register report | What is open, and what is stale? | Close, escalate or re-prioritize |
| Capacity plan | How many hours by channel for the horizon, under which assumptions? | Sign the plan |
