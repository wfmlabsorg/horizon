# HORIZON Mission

## Purpose

Run books of business as living ledgers, so that planners approve rather than type.

## Core Mission

HORIZON exists to show how a workforce planning organization runs its books of business when the
planning artifacts are versioned data structures instead of spreadsheets, and the daily work of
updating, reforecasting, explaining and answering is done by a team of specialist agents under a
coordinator, with a human gate on every change to a plan.

## What This Means

1. **Ledgers, not sheets.** Every planning object (demand, forecast, supply, events, questions,
   plans) is a versioned file with a change log and a definitions block. Nothing is overwritten.

2. **Every number carries a grade.** [M] measured, [C] computed, [E] estimated, [A] asserted.
   A number without a grade is not a number yet.

3. **Association is never presented as cause.** The PostAnalyst finds the miss and decomposes it
   (Rung 1). The CausalAnalyst draws the DAG and names the confounders (Rungs 2 and 3). The
   Evaluator blocks anything that skips a rung.

4. **Questions become knowledge.** Every "why" that arrives is answered once as a graded card and
   filed in the book's register where the next asker finds it.

5. **Planners approve; agents propose.** A reforecast, a capacity plan, a severity change or a
   publication reaches a human before it reaches the plan.

## What HORIZON Replaces

Three things people do in spreadsheets today:

- the daily update-and-reforecast
- the "why did it go bump" investigation
- the monthly capacity plan

It mimics the two real integration points, IEX (short-term forecast load) and Anaplan (long-term
plan), as adapters that read and write files in those shapes.

## Representation

HORIZON represents the Workforce Planning team's method: a demonstration that a planning
organization can run on graded ledgers, specialist agents and human gates, with a full audit
trail from a raw actual to a signed plan.

## Non-Goals

- HORIZON is not a general-purpose assistant
- HORIZON does not schedule agents or manage real-time adherence (the next layers down the stack)
- HORIZON does not integrate with live systems; IEX, Anaplan and email are file adapters
- HORIZON does not replace planner judgment; it prepares the decision and waits
