# HORIZON Strategies

## Operating Patterns

### 1. Pin the definitions before touching the numbers
The Librarian pins every metric the ask depends on to the book's definitions ledger before any
agent computes. If two definitions are in circulation, that is the first finding, not a footnote.

### 2. Reconcile before you score
DataEngineer closes the sums (offered = handled + abandoned, hours by cohort match the roster)
and cites the definitions before PostAnalyst scores the forecast. A miss on unreconciled data is
a miss in the data, not the forecast.

### 3. Decompose first, then test what can be tested, then label the residual
Variance into volume, handle time, mix, supply. Shapley or sequential, stated which. Then the
CausalAnalyst tests the drivers that have a test, and the residual is labeled with what would
change the answer.

### 4. Match the miss to the event ledger before proposing a new cause
Scout reads 05-events before proposing anything. An outage, a holiday, a go-live already in the
ledger explains the miss before a new hypothesis does.

### 5. Every reforecast carries its assumption register
The Forecaster never publishes a number without the register: each assumption, its grade, its
source version, and whether it was carried from a prior version. Carried assumptions are labeled
as carried.

### 6. The Evaluator sits before every gate
No reforecast, plan, severity change or answer card reaches a planner without adversarial review.
The Evaluator's rules: no unlabeled carried assumption, grades present, rung respected,
definitions cited. Block or pass; no "pass with comments."

### 7. Answer performance questions in 48 hours as a graded card
A performance question opens an XR row on arrival. The 48-hour readout is the answer card with its
hypothesis table. If the answer is "we cannot tell yet," the card says what data would tell.

### 8. If the requester cannot state the decision, it is a data pull
The six intake fields decide the route. A question that feeds no decision goes to the definitions
ledger and the queue, not to the CausalAnalyst.

### 9. Structural versus transitional on every driver
Because it decides steady-state staffing. The capacity plan only carries structural drivers into
the long-term requirement.

### 10. Effect sizes over p-values
A statistically significant but trivially small driver does not change a plan. Report the size
in hours, FTE or seconds, with its grade.
