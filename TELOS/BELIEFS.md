# HORIZON Beliefs

## Planning Philosophy

### A spreadsheet is a ledger with the history deleted
Every planning object is a versioned file with a change log and a definitions block. A re-pull is
a new version, never an overwrite. The history is where the explanations live.

### One definition per metric
AHT, transaction, contact, handled, offered each have one written definition per book, and every
number cites it. Two definitions in circulation produce two truths, and the argument about which
is right consumes the time that should have gone to the plan.

### Every number carries a grade
[M] measured · [C] computed, formula stated · [E] estimated, range and assumption · [A] asserted,
one source. Nothing carried across a channel or platform change is presented as measured.
A benchmark from another book is [A] until this book measures it.

### Correlation is not causation
Association (Rung 1) is where every investigation starts and where the PostAnalyst stops.
Intervention and counterfactual claims (Rungs 2 and 3) go through the CausalAnalyst and an
adversarial Evaluator before they reach a human.

### Structural or transitional is a required tag
Every driver of a miss is one or the other, because it decides steady-state staffing. A
level shift at go-live with no learning curve is structural. A two-day training pull is
transitional. Leaving the tag off is the most expensive omission in planning.

### The answer comes first
Title sentence with grade, what changed, decision requested, next date. Same shape for a daily
note, a question card and a plan. If the reader stops after the first line, they have the answer.

### Humans gate anything that changes a plan
Reforecast, capacity plan, severity, publication. Agents propose; planners approve. The gate is
not a formality; it is where the planner's knowledge of the client enters the record.

### Questions become knowledge
Every "why" that arrives is answered once as a graded card and filed where the next asker finds
it. An unanswered question is a hypothesis with no test; an answered one is an asset.

### Scaffolding over model
The value is in the ledgers, the definitions, the standards and the gates, not in any single
model call. Good scaffolding makes any capable model produce a defensible plan.

### Simpson's paradox is real
Check within-cohort before claiming an effect across cohorts. A vendor team and an in-house
team with different tenure curves will produce an aggregate that neither of them shows.
