# forecast-daily.csv

Plan of record v000: the original daily forecast by channel for days 1–120, built on the assumptions in `assumptions.md` (whole-book contacts-per-transaction of 0.35, Beacon AHT carried as **AHT (agent work)**, flat phase lines with a weekday shape and no seasonal ramp). Required productive hours are workload ÷ 0.85 occupancy.

## Provenance

Generated by `sim/generate.ts` (seed 20260720); synthetic data with a recorded ground truth in `sim/GROUND-TRUTH.md`. Regenerate with `bun run sim/generate.ts`. Rows: 360.
