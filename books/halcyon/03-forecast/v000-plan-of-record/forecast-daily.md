# forecast-daily.csv — v000 plan of record

Plan of record v000: the original daily forecast by region × channel for days 2–120, built on the assumptions in `assumptions.md` (whole-book contacts-per-transaction of 0.35 applied to every migrated region, Beacon handle times carried across the platform change, flat phase lines with a weekday shape and no seasonal ramp). Regions enter the plan on their go-live day (North day 2, East day 37).
