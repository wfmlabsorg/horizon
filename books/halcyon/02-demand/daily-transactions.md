# daily-transactions.csv

Transactions per day by region for the whole Halcyon book (all three regions, whatever platform they are on) with the platform each region is on that day. Definition: **Transaction** (a booking, change or cancellation completed in the travel system, independent of any contact).

## Provenance

Generated by `sim/generate.ts` (seed 20260720); synthetic data with a recorded ground truth in `sim/GROUND-TRUTH.md`. Regenerate with `bun run sim/generate.ts`. Rows: 360.
